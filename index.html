01About
“3+ years turning spatial data into decisions.”

Early-career geospatial researcher working at the intersection of GIScience, GeoAI, and spatial data science, with a research record spanning flood and disaster risk, environmental health, climate vulnerability and migration, and land-use dynamics. My thesis and publications apply remote sensing, geoinformatics, and machine learning to hazard and health risk assessment, while my ongoing academic and professional research extends across coastal and riverine systems, ecosystem monitoring, and UAV-based deep learning for high-resolution environmental mapping. This work reflects a broader interest in modelling human-environment interactions using multi-source geospatial data to measure how landscapes change, identify the drivers of those changes, and assess their impacts on exposed communities, with the aim of producing evidence that can inform disaster resilience and adaptation planning.
