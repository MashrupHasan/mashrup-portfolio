“From satellite to survey to solution.”
Research Coverage · Bangladesh

Geospatial researcher working at the intersection of GIScience, GeoAI, and spatial data science, with a research record spanning flood and disaster risk, environmental health, climate vulnerability and migration, land-use dynamics, and vision-based geospatial analytics. My research integrates remote sensing, geoinformatics, and machine learning to investigate human-environment interactions across terrestrial and coastal systems, developing scalable methods for environmental monitoring, hazard and health risk assessment, ecosystem analysis, and spatial decision support, in both academic research and applied policy work. The throughline is using multi-source geospatial data and AI to understand landscape change, quantify its drivers and impacts, and generate evidence that supports climate resilience, disaster risk reduction, and sustainable environmental management.
